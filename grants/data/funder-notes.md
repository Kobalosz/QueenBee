# Funder and Portal Notes

What runs have learned about specific funders and form platforms: eligibility traps, per-cycle
limits, form quirks, which pages block scripts, and any page text aimed at AI readers. Read it
before opening a funder's form. Add a dated line every time something costs a run.

Shape: `- YYYY-MM-DD · Funder or platform · what happened · what to do next time`

## Platforms
- Grants.gov · Submission needs the client's Login.gov account, a Grants.gov Workspace and the AOR
  role for the organization's UEI. The agent never touches any of it; it hands over a package.
- SAM.gov · Registration is free. A UEI alone is not an active registration; check the expiration
  date the client gives and flag it when it falls before an award date.
- Submittable · Many foundations host forms here. Each needs the client's own Submittable account
  and Chrome signed in; add `submittable.com` to `channels.signed_in_portals` once that is true.
